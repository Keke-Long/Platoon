\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper establishes a theoretically controlled preprocessing framework for vehicle scheduling at general conflict areas. Rather than replacing the downstream optimizer, the framework selects an aggregation level that reflects the central scheduler's computational resources and acceptable scheduling loss. The analytical guarantee is now stated as the FIFO-indexed bound in Section~\ref{sec:theory}; the final empirical conclusions will be written after the indexed verification code and large-scale numerical experiments are completed.
