\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper establishes a theoretically controlled preprocessing framework for vehicle scheduling at general conflict areas. The FIFO-indexed bound quantifies the worst-case average-delay loss caused by a contiguous platoon partition without imposing a probabilistic arrival assumption. The bound then guides selection of an aggregation level consistent with the scheduler's model-size budget and acceptable performance loss.

Exhaustive small-instance calculations found no violation of the analytical guarantee and included equality cases. The computational study further shows how the proposed rule reduces downstream ordering variables while preserving a checkable worst-case loss bound on solved instances. These results demonstrate that the indexed bound is useful not only as a theoretical guarantee but also as a practical guide for reducing downstream MILP dimension.
