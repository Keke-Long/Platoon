\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper establishes a theoretically controlled rule-based preprocessing method for vehicle scheduling at general conflict areas. The FIFO-indexed bound quantifies the worst-case average-delay loss caused by a contiguous platoon partition without imposing a probabilistic arrival assumption. The proposed PP rule uses a critical-headway threshold \(\delta\) and maximum platoon size \(P_{\max}\) to form same-approach platoons before the downstream scheduling MILP is constructed.

Exhaustive small-instance calculations found no violation of the analytical guarantee and included equality cases. The computational study further compares NP, CHP, and PP, showing how the proposed threshold-and-cap rule reduces downstream ordering variables while preserving a checkable worst-case loss bound on solved instances. These results demonstrate that the indexed bound is useful not only as a theoretical guarantee but also as a practical guide for reducing downstream MILP dimension.
